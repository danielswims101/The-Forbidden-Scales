The Forbidden
Scales

An epic dragon saga of loyalty, ancient power, and the bonds forged between unlikely companions — written by four voices, one world.

In a world where dragon bloodlines are destiny and elemental Shards define every hierarchy of power, five extraordinary dragons and their companions are about to discover that the greatest force is not the one born into you — it is the one you choose to become. The scales are tipping. The Balance is fraying. Something forbidden has been waking up for a very long time.

Dragon Records

Five dragons. Four authors. Every one of them a main character. Each written by a different member of the team, giving every dragon a wholly distinct voice, a wholly distinct history, and a wholly distinct weight to carry. Twilight belongs to all four authors together. These are not supporting characters. Every single one of them is the protagonist of their own story.

A
Agate
WindScale  ·  Queen's Regent  ·  Written by Elina
WindScalePescatarian Photographic MemoryQueen's Regent Bound to Saiber
Appearance
Immediately striking — scales a layered blend of tangerine orange and deep amber-gold, the colours shifting as she moves like something caught between fire and honey. Wings a softer clementine, underbelly faint apricot. Waves of golden scales march down her spine and forearms, catching light like armour she was born wearing. Claws are pure gold. Eyes are amber with bright gold flecks — lit from behind. She wears copper and bronze armbands, an amber necklace, and the long winding golden tailband of a high-ranking advisor.
Power & The Bond with Saiber
During intense emotion — real joy, grief, rage — Agate generates uncontrolled energy outbursts that command the wind. Early on, entirely involuntary and frightening. Later she learns to reach for it deliberately. At her strongest, the winds respond to the direction of her thoughts before she consciously commands them.

Her bond with Saiber is not simply companionship — it is a metaphysical and literal binding in the fantasy sense. Where Agate goes, Saiber follows. Where Saiber is threatened, her power responds before her mind does. He saved her life at her promotion ceremony by throwing himself at an attacker without being asked. Their bond is one of the oldest mysteries in the records.
Personality Arc & Role
Begins nervous and over-careful — triple-checks everything, apologises out of reflex, hides cleverness behind politeness. Over the series she becomes funny, warm, and startlingly calm under pressure — the kind of leader others follow without being asked, without her ever noticing. As Queen's Regent she holds enormous political power and almost no personal freedom. She is the emotional centre of the group.
T
Topaz
WindScale  ·  Prince  ·  Written by Daniel
WindScalePrince Snot WindBonded to Fang
Appearance
Deep tangerine scales shot through with dark gold flecks. Golden-brown eyes with a sharp intelligence that evaluates everything and gives away nothing. Winding golden tailband shaped as a coiled snake, whip-thin tail, claws sharp as obsidian, teeth like steel blades. A scar through the thick of his tail — origin never officially recorded. His pouch has never been opened in front of anyone. His mother's servants once prepared orange and cinnamon perfume for his nightmares; he told every visiting dragonet it was hers.
Power — The Snot Wind
Topaz can blast concentrated wind from his snout. Officially, permanently, and non-negotiably named Snot Wind by Daniel, the author, under direct threat of resignation from TFS if changed. Despite the undignified name it is genuinely devastating at close range — a focused burst of wind pressure that knocks a dragon twice his size off their feet, deflects projectiles, and disorients an opponent's balance completely before Topaz follows with something worse.
Personality & History
Bitter, haughty, and protective in ways he would never voluntarily admit. He has been let down before — by people he trusted, by the idea that royalty meant safety — and constructed armour of arrogance around that history so effectively that most people never get past it. He has what the records describe as moon eyes for Agate — which he would deny, loudly, unconvincingly. He killed his own father to save Eclipse. He broke down immediately after. He is not over it.
The Bond with Fang
Topaz fled his home and stumbled into the territory of Fang's Red Wolf pack. They made eye contact and both felt something neither can explain — a recognition between their temperaments. Fang always knows where Topaz is. Fang can run at the speed of sound for 5,000 miles, growl approximations of Dragon words, and call his entire pack from anywhere on the continent. Topaz has never once asked him to leave.
T
Talon
ShardScale  ·  Servant of Ky  ·  Written by Raven
ShardScaleVegan No PowerBonded to Dyno
Appearance
Small and scrawny — easily overlooked, which suits him. Light icy-blue scales, dark blue dots across the underside of his wings like a constellation pressed into his skin. Electric-blue eyes sharper than they first appear. A small leather pouch around his neck — what's inside has never been explained. A crystal dagger with a smooth rock handle, watermelon tourmaline frozen into it, and the inscription: Failure is not trying, whilst winning is putting up a fight.
Power — None
In a world entirely defined by elemental power, Talon has nothing. No ability. No domain. No gift from his bloodline. He has survived entirely through wit, adaptability, and a stubbornness that refuses to let circumstances be the end of the story. Whether this makes him the weakest member of the group or the most dangerous depends entirely on who you ask. Several characters have made the mistake of assuming the former.
History & Personality
Parents and sister taken just after hatching. Forced to pass tests to prove his worth to survive the palace. His sister became the Queen's favourite and unrecognisable. He found Aquamarine through a collision and shattered glass — she became his first family. Then he found Ky. Then the others. He does not believe in luck. He believes in people. Fiercely protective of Dyno and most of all of Ky, whom he regards not as a master but as a brother.

Ash
MoonScale  ·  Secret Heir  ·  Written by Varsha
MoonScaleRoyalty Night-PoweredBonded to Storm Eclipse's Cousin
Appearance
Black and deep navy-blue in a gradient that fades seamlessly into shadow — in dim light nearly invisible, which is often deliberate. Tiny silver stars scattered across her wings like actual sky pressed into membrane. Blue eyes with depth that seems greater than it should, as though there is more light behind them than in front. In full moonlight she is breathtaking. In daylight she looks like something that belongs elsewhere.
Power & Status
Draws power directly from the moon — strength rises and falls with the lunar cycle. At full moon, formidable. In direct sunlight, diminished. Technically in line to the MoonScale throne after Princess Cyllene and Prince Nighthunter — but she is a runaway, hiding what she is. She carves weapons from wood in the dark. She carries citrine stones. She carved Eclipse's face from memory and embedded the jewels as eyes.
Personality & Bond with Storm
Guarded with strangers — earned caution, not unkindness. Get past that wall and find someone fun, intensely driven, and frighteningly smart, with a dry humour that catches people off guard. She says nothing she does not mean. Ash and Storm communicate in feelings and images across enormous distances. Storm's hunting instinct and Ash's strategic thinking have never once conflicted.

Twilight
GalaxyScale  ·  "Twiy"  ·  Shared by all four authors
GalaxyScaleWanted Dual DomainBonded to Angel
Appearance
Pink and purple scales that shift and catch light differently depending on angle — like a gemstone that refuses to be singular. A dab of royal blue at the end of her tail. Rose-gold claws. And her eyes: look closely and there is a star-shaped marking in the iris, a birthmark in the shape of a constellation. It does not move. It does not change. It has been there since she hatched.
Power
As a GalaxyScale, Twilight draws from the galaxy and stars simultaneously — light and dark in equal measure. During extreme emotion she releases uncontrolled blasts of raw power difficult to predict and harder to stop. After being rescued from the SeaScale dungeons by Princess Pearl, she said: "The stars will shine bright tonight. But you will not live to see them." A beam of pure starlight struck her attacker down and burned them to ash. She was horrified. She did not know she could do that.
Personality & Status
Kind without conditions. Adventurous to a fault. Constitutionally incapable of being anything other than completely honest even when honesty is the least convenient option. Funny, generous, recklessly clumsy. She calls herself Twiy. Currently wanted by an unknown faction for reasons not yet disclosed. She does not know yet. The people who do know have decided, for now, not to tell her.
The Forbidden Scales — The Books

Twenty main volumes. Fifteen special stories and editions. Each book building on the last, each one a step deeper into a world that keeps revealing new rooms. No spoilers. Only promises — and hooks designed to keep you up past midnight.

Book One
The Heartstonian Friendship
Five dragons from different tribes, different ranks, different worlds — thrown together by circumstances none of them chose. Before the powers, before the politics, before everything else: the moment that will either save or doom all of them.
The world needs them to trust each other. The world has not told them this yet.
Book Two
A Revival
Something believed to be over is not over. Something believed to be dead is not dead. The group, barely held together, must contend with a threat that should not exist — and the discovery that some of what they were told about the world was deliberately, carefully wrong.
They buried it. They did not bury it deep enough.
Book Three
The True Awakening
Powers begin to shift. Things dormant come online. Things assumed understood turn out to be only partly understood. For some, this is a gift. For others, it is the thing they were most afraid of.
The power was always there. The question is what it will do now that it is awake.
Book Four
Heated Rebellion
Someone has had enough. A rebellion is building — not only among dragons, but within the group itself. Loyalties that seemed certain are tested. Old alliances crack. Fire, literal and metaphorical, is everywhere.
You can only push someone so far. They pushed too far.
Book Five
The Fractured Crown
Royalty has never been safe, and now it is not even stable. The thrones of the dragon world are cracking — politically, physically, personally. The question of who deserves power, and who has simply inherited it, becomes unavoidable.
The crown was never a gift. It was always a trap.
Book Six
Shards of the Forgotten
History returns. Ancient Shard bloodlines believed extinct begin to resurface — and with them, answers to questions the group has been asking since Book One. The world is older than anyone told them. The Balance has been broken before.
They erased the records. They forgot that some of the records walked away.
Book Seven
When Stars Burn Dark
Twilight and Ash step into the foreground. The galaxy and the moon — light and dark, star and shadow — their arcs converge here in ways neither anticipated. This is the book about what it means to carry power you did not ask for.
She stopped running. The darkness had been waiting for exactly that moment.
Book Eight
The Last Correspondence
The Wheel is cracking. The Balance — the ancient system of opposing powers that keeps the world stable — is failing, and the group is at the centre of it. The consequences of everything since Book One arrive all at once.
The letters stopped arriving. That means one of two things. Neither one is good.
Book Nine
Eclipse of the Forbidden
Eclipse lives. Eclipse has always lived. And now Eclipse is no longer in the background. The most powerful dragon in the series steps forward — and the group must decide what side of that event they want to be on.
She told them she was gone. She never said it was permanent.
Book Ten
The Final Scale
The end of the first half. What was broken, what was built, what was lost, and what remains. Every main character gets an ending that is true to who they are. Whether that ending is happy depends on what you consider happiness.
The scale tips. The world holds its breath. Then the second half begins.
Book Eleven
Blood of the Unbroken
Something survived the events of Book Ten that should not have. The group, scattered and changed, is forced to reunite — but the people who come back are not the same people who separated. They have been remade by what happened to them.
The scar healed wrong. Now it has to be broken open again.
Book Twelve
The Hollow Throne
The seat of power in the dragon world is empty. Every faction wants to fill it. Every faction has a reason no other faction should. The group discovers the throne was not empty by accident.
Empty seats are not vacant. They are invitations to the wrong kind of dragon.
Book Thirteen
The Starless Age
The sky goes dark. Not metaphorically — all at once, something extinguishes the stars. Both Sun Shards and Galaxy Shards feel it in their bones. The threat stops being political and becomes something much, much older.
The stars are not gone. They are being held somewhere. The question is by what.
Book Fourteen
The War of the Wheel
The Correspondence — the ancient balance of opposing Shard bloodlines — goes to war with itself. Shard against Shard, ancient law against ancient law. When the elements fight each other, the world is the battlefield.
When the elements fight each other, the world is the battlefield.
Book Fifteen
Shadow and Ruin
ShadowHunter. The spirit that possessed Eclipse, that haunted the group across continents. This is her book. The history no one knew. The reason she is what she is. Understanding her will not make her safe. But it might make her stoppable.
She was not always this. Something made her this. That something still exists.
Book Sixteen
The Drowned Kingdom
Deep beneath the ocean there is a kingdom the SeaScales do not talk about and the other tribes do not know exists. What they find changes the history of every tribe on the continent.
They call it drowned. The dragons inside it call it home.
Book Seventeen
Children of the Galaxy
The GalaxyScales have no queen, no official territory, no throne. Goldstone holds them together through sheer force of will and something older than politics. This book is their story — where they came from, why they hide, what they are waiting for.
Every tribe has a history. The GalaxyScales have a secret instead.
Book Eighteen
The Price of the Heartstone
The Heartstone — created by all tribes' Shards, said to be the greatest object of power — is found. What it costs to use it, who has been keeping it, and what it was actually built to do are three completely different answers from what anyone expected.
They said it could save the world. They didn't say what it would cost.
Book Nineteen
The Age Before
Told entirely in the past. The world fifteen years ago. Night and Luna. The GalaxyScales' secret home before it was revealed. The first meeting that started everything. Context for every single thing that has happened since Book One.
Now you know why. Now go back and reread everything.
Book Twenty
The Forbidden Scales
The final book. The same title as the series. The Wheel either turns again or it does not. The Balance either holds or it breaks permanently. Every character who has made it this far gets the ending they have been moving toward since the beginning.
It was always going to end here. They just didn't know which way the scales would tip.

Special Story XI

Special Story XII
Cobalt's Promise
Cobalt, who snuck Ky snacks after Lapis's lessons, who built a tent in a secret spot so a shy dragonet would have somewhere safe. The full story of what he knew that he never told Ky, and why he kept it.

An in-universe historical document — the scholarly record of the Heartstone's creation, its uses through history, the attempts to destroy it, and the debates among every tribe's historians about what it actually is. Contains more truth than it appears to.

The five crystal gifts found in the hidden clearing — Comfort, Endurance, Prosperity, Light, and Dreams. The Gift of Power, missing. This story traces each gift: where it came from, who made it, and why someone removed the sixth before anyone could find it.

The Forbidden Archives
The collector's edition — deleted scenes, alternate chapters, author notes, early drafts, original character concepts, and in-world documents that never made it into the main books. Includes Ky's original journal entry that someone wrote in without his knowledge.
Scales of War

A companion series set in the same world, running through the same wars, but following an entirely different cast across the six kingdoms. While the main series follows five young dragons discovering who they are, Scales of War follows warriors discovering what the world costs. Every event ties directly into the mythology, politics, and lore of The Forbidden Scales. Same world. Other side of the battlefield.

Scales of War
By Daniel Lim  ·  Companion series to The Forbidden Scales
Scales of War follows warriors, generals, and council members trying to end a war that has run long enough to become simply the world as it exists. It begins with two FlameScale trainees and their queen, expands to six warriors from six tribes, and ends with all of them sitting across a table trying to accomplish what armies never could: peace. The Heartstone — mentioned in the main series — is the central object of power here, created by all tribes' Shards working together.
Anile
FlameScale  ·  FlameShard  ·  Main Character
Lance
Daughter of Eidas, the greatest traitor in FlameScale history — a stigma that has followed her since before she could fly. Despite this, she is the most powerful dragonet in her training cohort and a full FlameShard who can transform into living fire. Queen Firetalon personally witnessed her powers manifest and invited her to train directly under the queen's supervision. She is proving that being someone's daughter does not determine who you are.
Anred

The most powerful fighter in their squad — the kind of companion who shows up without being asked and leaves when the problem is solved. When Firetalon invited Anile, he came too. He carries a spear. He is very good with it. He is better at friendship.

General of the SeaScale army, Queen Angelfish's right hand, and a SeaShard. He met Anile and Anred during a joint expedition to capture a traitor who had been evading both kingdoms. They fought on the same side without planning to, and realised somewhere in the middle of it that they were better together than apart. He carries a trident and is responsible for explaining what Shards are to several characters who had never encountered the term.

Ephant
GalaxyScale  ·  GalaxyShard  ·  Side Character
None Recorded
A GalaxyScale and GalaxyShard — one of the rarest combinations alive. Ephant knew Eclipse. They were connected before the main series began, though the nature of that connection is not yet in the official files. Ephant also lost siblings — how, and to what, is part of what Scales of War is building toward. Operates under Goldstone, de facto leader of the GalaxyScales. Galaxy Shards rarely need a recorded weapon.
Queen Firetalon — Monarch of the FlameScales
The most powerful FlameScale alive and the reigning monarch of the FlameScale kingdom. She personally intervened when Anile's powers manifested and an admiral used violence against a training dragonet. Her exact words: "I promote strength and discipline. But this is neither. This is the opposite of what I stand for." She demoted the admiral to common soldier on the spot and invited Anile to train directly under her. She is calm the way only someone who has survived a very long time can be — not because she is unthreatened, but because she has already assessed every threat and chosen her response before anyone else noticed there was one. She knows what a FlameShard is. She was one of the first.
The Shards

The most powerful dragon bloodlines in recorded history. Each Shard commands absolute dominion over a fundamental force of the world — not as a skill, not as a learned ability, but as a birthright written into their scales at the moment of hatching. Old scholars called them Shards because they believed each bloodline was a fragment broken from something far greater — a primordial power too vast for any single being to hold whole. They appear once every century, sometimes less. They exist in both series. No two are equivalent. None are safe to underestimate.

Flame
Domain of Fire & Heat
Core Power
A Flame Shard can transform their entire body into living fire — not a coating or an aura, but a full physical conversion into something between dragon and star. The flames burn at temperatures so extreme they can melt any known metal on contact. Stone becomes liquid glass. Ice does not melt — it ceases to exist. When Anile's powers first manifested, she set her training grounds ablaze before she understood what she was. Queen Firetalon was the one who reached into her fiery consciousness and helped her find the off switch.
Extended Abilities
Can command fire from any source within a wide radius. Completely fireproof. Advanced Flame Shards ignite the air itself, creating pressure waves of burning force that knock enemies flat before the fire arrives. They leave scorched glass footprints at full power. The most gifted create sustained infernos requiring no fuel — fire that burns indefinitely, without consuming anything.
Known — Anile (SOW) · Queen Firetalon (SOW)
Sea
Domain of Water & Ice
Core Power
Sea Shards command water in every form — liquid, ice, vapour, and the transitional states between. They can pull moisture from the air itself, drawing from clouds, stone, even the exhalations of living things. Their senses are calibrated so precisely to water that they can detect any source within miles: underground rivers, rainfall before clouds form, hidden reservoirs. What they find, they control. What they control, they weaponise.
Extended Abilities
Can freeze water instantly — rivers into walls, rain into falling blades, waves into cages. Advanced Sea Shards freeze the moisture in the air around a target, building a prison mid-motion. They breathe underwater indefinitely. Most unsettlingly: they can sense the water content of living bodies — and the most powerful have learned to use this knowledge in ways not documented in public records.
Known — Leinad (SOW)
Moon
Domain of Darkness & Perception
Core Power
Moon Shards do not destroy — they unmake the ability to perceive. They can reach into an enemy's visual field and darken their sight completely — lasting from seconds to minutes. They conjure spheres of pure, absolute darkness that expand like a held breath releasing, swallowing areas whole. Inside these zones, nothing can be seen and sound bends away from its source.
Extended Abilities
Linked directly to the lunar cycle — at full moon near-unstoppable, at new moon significantly weakened. Can muffle sound within darkness zones. Advanced Moon Shards create a shadow-self — a duplicate made of darkness that moves independently and mirrors their actions. The most powerful sustain darkness that persists after they leave, fed by moonlight alone. They see perfectly in any darkness.
Known — Ahsrav (SOW) · Ash draws moon power — Shard status unconfirmed
Crystal
Domain of Earth & Armour
Core Power
Crystal Shards can manifest armour from nothing — pulling mineral matter from earth, stone, or minerals dissolved in air itself, forming perfectly-structured crystalline plates in seconds. This armour is not rock: it is harder than anything produced in any forge, structured at a molecular level to distribute impact instead of absorbing it. They can also command the earth — shift stone, raise walls, crack ground open with surgical precision.
Extended Abilities
Can extend armour into spikes, shields, blades, and barriers — wearing the battlefield as an extension of their body. If armour shatters, they simply pull more from the ground beneath them. They can sense structural weaknesses in anything solid — walls, terrain, armour on other dragons. The most powerful have been recorded encasing opponents in crystal entirely, freezing them mid-motion in a transparent prison.
Known — Nevar (SOW)
Wind
Domain of Air & Motion
Core Power
Wind Shards bend the air entirely to their will — from a current gentle enough to carry a whisper miles away to a sustained gale that levels forests. The wind is not only a weapon: it is a sixth sense. They feel every disturbance in the air around them — movement, approaching bodies, even emotional states read through the subtle shifts in what a living creature exhales. They are nearly impossible to ambush. They know you are coming before you decide to come.
Extended Abilities
Can fly at speeds that make them nearly impossible to track visually. Can create localised vacuums — pockets with no air — effective against fire and suffocating against anything that breathes. Elite Wind Shards create contained storms: vortexes that trap opponents, twisters that scour terrain, walls of wind that deflect everything including sound. Agate's emotion-triggered outbursts share qualities with a Wind Shard at partial strength — her Shard status is unconfirmed.
Known — Crande (SOW) · Agate — Wind-Shard-adjacent, unconfirmed
Sun
Domain of Light & Radiance
Core Power
Sun Shards can unleash blinding flashes of concentrated light — not ordinary brightness but focused photonic force that overwhelms visual processing entirely, leaving enemies sightless for extended periods. They can also become a living beacon, radiating brilliance visible from impossible distances. Their light is directional, concentrated, and capable of revealing anything hidden in shadow or obscured by Moon Shard power.
Extended Abilities
Draw directly from sunlight — dramatically stronger at midday and in open terrain. Can focus radiance into a narrow beam of concentrated solar energy capable of cutting through stone. Advanced Sun Shards maintain light constructs: sustained walls of radiance that hurt to approach, illuminated decoys, persistent beacons. Some scholars believe powerful Sun Shards can accelerate biological processes using focused light — healing, growth, and in theory, the reverse. Their power always announces their location. This is the price.
Known — Mantre (SOW)
Galaxy
The Dual Shard  ·  Domain of All Light & All Dark
Full Moon Powers Full Sun Powers Dual Domain Extremely Rare Hunted
What Makes Them Different
Galaxy Shards carry the full power of the Moon and the full power of the Sun simultaneously in one body, without cancelling each other out. Old scholars believed this impossible — that light and dark in equal measure would neutralise into nothing. Instead they compound. A Galaxy Shard can flood a battlefield with blinding radiance and snuff every light source in existence in the same breath. They are not a compromise. They are both, entire.
The Unique Ability — Spatial Perception
Because Galaxy Shards command both what can be seen and what cannot, they develop something unique: spatial perception. An awareness of the space between light and dark. In practice: they detect cloaked or invisible things that even Moon Shards miss, track the path of light bending around obstacles. Advanced Galaxy Shards can bend light around their own form, rendering themselves invisible not in darkness but in plain sight. Twilight generated both a blinding flash and a total darkness field simultaneously in the SeaScale dungeons, creating complete sensory disorientation. She burned her attacker to ash. She did not know she could do that.
Known — Twilight (TFS) · Ephant (SOW) · Eclipse — status complicated
No other single Shard type can fully counter a Galaxy Shard. Moon Shards cannot use darkness against them — they share it. Sun Shards cannot blind them — they invented the ability. This is not a power that should exist in one dragon. This is why they are hunted. This is why Goldstone hides her people.
The New Tribes

Seven additional dragon tribes, each with their own domain, their own queen, and their own Shard bloodline. These tribes expand the world of The Forbidden Scales and Scales of War significantly — widening the Wheel of Correspondence, deepening the political map, and introducing new forces that the Balance must account for. Every one of them was always there. The records are only now catching up.

StormScale
Domain of Lightning & Tempest  ·  Queen Stormbringer
Core Power
Storm Shards command lightning and the full fury of tempests. They can call down lightning from a clear sky, channel electrical current through their own body without harm, and generate localised storms that respond to their emotional state. In battle, a Storm Shard moves like weather itself — unpredictable, omnidirectional, and impossible to fully anticipate. Their strikes arrive before the sound of them does.
Extended Abilities
Advanced Storm Shards can ride their own lightning — moving at electrical speed across short distances, appearing to teleport in combat. They can magnetise objects, disrupt Crystal armour through electromagnetic force, and create persistent storm systems that remain active long after they leave an area. Their roar can carry the force of a thunderclap. Their presence causes the air to taste like metal.
Opposes — Rune Shard (Chaos vs Order)
FrostScale
Domain of Cold & Stillness  ·  Queen Frostbite
Core Power
Frost Shards do not simply freeze water — they command cold itself, the absence of heat as a force in its own right. Where Sea Shards freeze what they find, Frost Shards create cold from nothing, lowering temperatures in ways that have nothing to do with water availability. They can freeze the air, the ground, the warmth inside a living body. They are entirely immune to cold and feel temperature as information rather than sensation.
Extended Abilities
Advanced Frost Shards create blizzard fields that reduce visibility to nothing and drop temperatures fast enough to crack stone. They can flash-freeze targets mid-motion — not by surrounding them with ice but by pulling the heat directly out of them. The most powerful Frost Shards have been recorded freezing time itself in small areas — a localised stillness where nothing moves until the cold releases it.
Opposes — Grove Shard (Death vs Life)
GroveScale
Domain of Nature & Growth  ·  Queen Thornbloom
Core Power
Grove Shards command all living plant matter — roots, vines, trees, moss, spores, and the unseen network that connects them beneath the ground. They can accelerate growth to impossible speeds, calling walls of thorns from bare earth, sending roots through stone, and communicating through the soil network faster than any messenger. A Grove Shard in a forest is never alone — the forest itself is their body.
Extended Abilities
Advanced Grove Shards can animate trees as weapons, direct root systems to restrain opponents underground before they appear, and accelerate plant growth to the point of structural collapse — a building, a wall, a throne can be consumed in minutes. They can also heal wounds using concentrated plant compounds. The most gifted Grove Shards can merge temporarily with a tree, moving through the grove's root network to emerge anywhere in the forest.
Opposes — Frost Shard (Life vs Death)
PrismScale
Domain of Light Refraction & Illusion  ·  Queen Prismara
Core Power
Prism Shards do not simply produce light — they bend, split, and weaponise it. Where Sun Shards radiate, Prism Shards refract, turning a single beam of light into a dozen cutting edges simultaneously. Their scales act as living glass, and they can focus ambient light into concentrated cutting beams capable of slicing through stone from a distance. They also construct perfect illusions — not darkness like Moon Shards, but convincing false realities made of bent light.
Extended Abilities
Advanced Prism Shards can create mirror-doubles of themselves and others using refracted light — copies so convincing they respond to attacks. They can render an area into pure chromatic confusion, splitting every light source into a blinding rainbow of cutting edges. The most powerful have been recorded building light-prisons — structures made of refracted beams that cut anything that passes through them from any direction.
Opposes — Mist Shard (Clarity vs Obscurement)
RuneScale
Domain of Order & Inscription  ·  Queen Runespire
Core Power
Rune Shards inscribe power. They can carve living runes into any surface — stone, earth, air, even the skin of other dragons — and those runes do exactly what they are written to do. A rune of binding holds. A rune of breaking destroys. A rune of silence seals sound. The limitation is that Rune Shards must know exactly what they are writing, and the precision required is extraordinary. Imprecision produces unpredictable results. Most Rune Shards are the most careful, methodical fighters in the world.
Extended Abilities
Advanced Rune Shards can inscribe delayed runes — traps that activate on a condition, barriers that hold until a specific force is applied, doors that open only for specific bloodlines. The most powerful have written runes into the ground of battlefields, activating them simultaneously when the fight reaches maximum chaos. They are the only Shard type capable of permanently sealing another Shard's power — temporarily, and at enormous personal cost.
Opposes — Storm Shard (Order vs Chaos)
AshScale
Domain of Decay & Ember  ·  Queen Ashbloom
Core Power
Ash Shards command what remains after destruction — the ash, smoke, embers, and slow decay that follow fire without being fire. They occupy the space between Flame and Moon on the Wheel, sharing qualities of both without belonging fully to either. They can generate choking ash clouds that obscure vision and poison breath, call embers from long-cold fires, and accelerate the decay of organic matter at terrifying speed. They are what happens after the Flame Shard leaves.
Extended Abilities
Advanced Ash Shards can ride smoke — dissolving partially into a cloud of ash and reforming elsewhere, making them extremely difficult to physically engage. They can cause armour, weapons, and structures to corrode at an accelerated rate. The most powerful Ash Shards have been recorded inducing decay in the ground itself — causing everything built on it to slowly collapse from underneath, without a single direct strike.
Sits between Flame and Moon on the Wheel — partial opposition to both
MistScale
Domain of Fog & Vanishing  ·  Queen Mistveil
Core Power
Mist Shards command fog — not water, not air, but the precise threshold between them where visibility collapses and the world loses its edges. They can generate total whiteout fog in seconds, render entire battlefields sightless without the absolute darkness of a Moon Shard, and make objects, dragons, and landmarks simply vanish into the mist as if they were never there. Where Prism Shards create false clarity, Mist Shards create false absence.
Extended Abilities
Advanced Mist Shards can sustain fog indefinitely over large areas without maintaining active concentration. They can move through their own mist without disorientation — their senses adapt to it, making them perfectly functional in conditions that incapacitate everyone else. The most powerful Mist Shards have been recorded causing dragons to lose themselves entirely inside a mist bank — not physically harmed, simply unable to find their way out until the Shard chooses to release them.
Opposes — Prism Shard (Obscurement vs Clarity)

The Wheel of Correspondence — Visual

All thirteen Shard bloodlines mapped across the Wheel. Opposing forces face each other across the centre. Adjacent forces share resonance. Galaxy sits outside the Wheel entirely — not at the centre, but beyond it, as a correction rather than a participant. The new tribes expand the outer ring; the original six form the inner ring of the oldest known oppositions.

The Great Balance

Nothing in the world of The Forbidden Scales exists without its opposite. The ancient scholars called it the Correspondence — the principle that the world maintains itself not through peace, but through opposition. Every power has a mirror. Every strength has a counter. When one Shard rises unchecked, another is drawn toward it like tide to shore. This is not coincidence. It is a law as old as the bloodlines themselves. And like all laws, it can be broken.

Flame
opposes
Sea
The oldest opposition. Flame burns hot enough to unmake metal — but Sea does not simply douse it. A Sea Shard can freeze the moisture in the air around a Flame Shard before the fire can reach full temperature, encasing them in ice during the window of vulnerability. Flame Shards at peak power evaporate water faster than it arrives — but Sea Shards draw from the atmosphere itself, from underground rivers, from clouds miles distant. The contest is not about which runs hotter or colder. It is about which runs out first.

Compound interaction: Flame and Wind together are catastrophic — fire fed by air becomes a spreading inferno a Wind Shard can direct at will. Sea and Crystal reinforce each other — water directed into earth channels, earth holding water in formations a Sea Shard could not maintain alone.
"The sea does not fear the fire. The sea simply remembers that fire needs air — and the sea has drowned things before they could ever burn." — Scholar's marginalia, volume and author unknown.
Sun
opposes
Moon
The most personal opposition. Sun and Moon Shards do not simply cancel each other's abilities — they unravel each other's entire purpose. A Moon Shard's darkness sphere, which can swallow a battlefield and leave armies helpless, is shattered instantly by a single focused Sun burst. A Sun Shard's blinding flash — terrifying against every other type — washes harmlessly over a Moon Shard who sees perfectly in absolute dark.

What makes this opposition extraordinary: in full daylight the Moon Shard weakens; at night the Sun Shard dims. They are calibrated against the world's own rhythm — day and night, the cycle that predates every dragon bloodline. They were not made to destroy each other. They were made to balance each other. Which is why the Galaxy Shard — who carries both — is not a balance. It is what happens when the system that requires a balance no longer functions. A Galaxy Shard is not a mediator. It is a correction.
"Light makes darkness visible. Darkness makes light meaningful. Take either away entirely and you get blindness of a different kind." — Recovered text, author unknown, estimated to predate the current tribal system.
Wind
opposes
Crystal
The opposition between the untethered and the immovable. Wind is formless, invisible, omnipresent — it cannot be held, caught, or permanently stopped. Crystal is the inverse: dense, permanent, built to endure. A Wind Shard can crack Crystal armour with enough sustained directional force — stone fractures under pressure if you know the angle. A Crystal Shard can drive itself into the earth and become immovable, removing the Wind Shard's primary tactic of throwing things around.

The tension is mobility versus durability. Wind Shards are the fastest combatants in the world. Crystal Shards are the most protected. In a sustained engagement Crystal wins — attrition favours armour. In a fast engagement Wind wins — speed favours evasion. This is why Wind Shards never let a fight become sustained. And why Crystal Shards always try to make it one.
"The mountain does not chase the wind. It does not need to. Every storm passes. The mountain is still there." — Traditional Crystal Shard proverb, used in combat training for several generations.
The Full Wheel & The Galaxy Exception
The six base Shards form the Wheel of Correspondence. No point has no counter. No two adjacent powers exist without tension. The direct oppositions: Flame and Sea · Sun and Moon · Wind and Crystal. The Wheel also turns across adjacencies: Flame and Wind compound into inferno. Sea and Crystal reinforce into fortified terrain. Sun and Flame share warmth — neither harms the other, together relentless. Moon and Sea share the tide — Sea Shards near Moon Shards report expanded range and heightened sensitivity.
The Galaxy Exception
The Galaxy Shard sits entirely outside the Wheel. Early scholars placed it at the centre — holding both light and dark, seeming like the keeper of balance. The more recent and uncomfortable theory: the Galaxy Shard is not the centre of the Wheel. It is what appears when the Wheel stops turning. Every several centuries, when one Shard bloodline grows too dominant or another nears extinction — a Galaxy Shard is born. Not as a ruler. Not as a hero. As a correction mechanism. Whether this is reassuring or terrifying depends entirely on which side of the imbalance you are standing on when it arrives.

The Wolf Pack

The Laws of the World
No omnipotent characters — Eclipse is the standing exception, and she still has consequences.
No all-powerful magic — wishing for anything to come into being is prohibited by the world's own rules.
No mythicals except dragons — the wolves are extraordinary but not mythical.
Body limits apply — every dragon must fit their Shard type's established physical range.
Four pages minimum per chapter — no exceptions granted.
Companions stay companions — they are part of the story, not the main characters.
The Heartstone
Created by all the tribes' Shards working together, the Heartstone is said to be one of the greatest objects of power ever made. Its creation required every Shard bloodline to contribute — making it the only object in the world that holds the power of all seven domains simultaneously. What it was built to do, who has been keeping it, and what it costs to use it are central questions in Scales of War. The answers are not what anyone expected. They connect directly to why the Balance has been fraying.

ShadowHunter — What She Is
ShadowHunter is not a dragon. She is something older — a spirit capable of possessing a body once given access to even a small portion of the host's soul. Eclipse, using dark power in a desperate moment, offered Shadow a corner of darkness. Shadow accepted and settled in, waiting. When Eclipse died, Shadow took full control of her body — seams visible where the corpse was stitched back together, eyes reflecting the universe but cold. She is the primary antagonist of the middle section of the series, and she is genuinely enjoying herself.
