The Forbidden
Scales

An epic dragon saga of loyalty, ancient power, and the bonds forged between unlikely companions

In a world where dragon bloodlines determine destiny, five young dragons and their companions must navigate war, loyalty, and power greater than any of them understand. The Scales are many. The forbidden ones are few — and the most dangerous of all.

Dragon Records

The five main dragons of the series — each written by a different author, each carrying the weight of their tribe, their power, and their story.

A
Agate
WindScale · Queen's Regent · Written by Elina
WindScale Pescatarian Photographic Memory Queen's Regent
Appearance: Amber eyes with glittering gold flecks in her irises. Her scales are a rich blend of tangerine orange and amber-gold, with lighter clementine wings and a faint apricot underbelly. Waves of gold scales shimmer down her spine and forearms, finished with gleaming golden claws.

Power: During extreme emotions — joy, grief, rage — Agate produces uncontrolled energy outbursts that command the wind around her. As she grows, she learns to channel this force deliberately, turning storms into shields and gales into weapons.

Personality Arc: She begins nervous, overthinking every step, hiding cleverness behind politeness. Over time she becomes funny, outgoing, calm under pressure — the kind of leader others follow without being asked. Her photographic memory makes her invaluable, but it also means she never forgets a wound.

Role: Acts as the Queen's regent — a position that grants her power and chains her equally. Her loyalty to those she loves is her greatest strength and her most dangerous vulnerability.
T
Topaz
WindScale · Prince · Written by Daniel
WindScale Prince Snot Wind
Appearance: Deep tangerine scales shot through with dark gold flecks. Golden-brown eyes with a sharp intelligence that misses nothing. A winding golden tail-band shaped like a coiled snake, a whip-thin tail, claws sharp as obsidian, teeth like steel blades — and a scar carved into the thick of his tail from a fight he refuses to speak of.

Power: Can blast concentrated wind from his snout — officially recorded as Snot Wind by the author and non-negotiable under pain of Daniel quitting TFS entirely. Despite the undignified name, the ability is genuinely devastating in battle at close range.

Personality: Bitter and haughty on the surface, protective and quietly loyal beneath. He has zero patience for disrespect directed at those he cares about, and moon eyes for Agate that he would never, ever admit to. His arrogance is partly armour — a prince who trusted too easily and learned not to.

Bond: His wolf Fang is his closest companion — they share a silent, instinctive understanding that bypasses language. Topaz will never say he needs anyone. Fang knows anyway.
T
Talon
ShardScale · Servant of Ky · Written by Raven
ShardScale Vegan No Power
Appearance: Small and scrawny — easily overlooked, which is exactly how he prefers it. Light icy-blue scales, dark blue dots scattered across the underside of his wings, electric blue eyes that are sharper than they seem. A small pouch hangs around his neck — what's inside is his secret alone.

Power: None. In a world defined by elemental power, Talon has nothing. He has survived entirely through wit, loyalty, and sheer stubbornness — which arguably makes him more dangerous than a dragon with every ability in the world.

Personality: Profoundly shy with everyone except the people he loves. Fiercely protective of Dyno, of Saiber, and most of all of Ky — whom he regards as a brother rather than a master. He does not fight because he wants glory. He fights because losing the people he loves is not something he's willing to experience again.

History: After his parents and sister were taken from him just after hatching, Talon was forced to prove his worth to survive. He did. He found Aquamarine. He found Ky. And he decided that family is chosen, not born.
A
Ash
MoonScale · Royalty · Written by Varsha
MoonScale Royalty Night-Powered
Appearance: Black and navy-blue scales that blend seamlessly into shadow, with tiny silver stars scattered across her wings like a section of real sky peeled free. Blue eyes that seem to hold depth beyond their surface. In darkness, she is nearly invisible. In moonlight, she is breathtaking.

Power: Draws power directly from the moon — which means she is strongest at night and grows weaker under heavy sunlight. Her abilities are moonlight-type: subtle, vast, and difficult to counter. She can perceive things others cannot, especially in darkness.

Personality: Uneasy and guarded when you first meet her — she has learned caution through experience, not paranoia. Get past that wall and you find someone fun, intensely hardworking, and fiercely smart. She says little and misses nothing.

Bond: Storm, her wolf companion, is the only being on earth that Ash fully trusts. They can understand each other across a distance neither of them can explain. Ash's plans and Storm's instincts have never once conflicted.

Twilight
GalaxyScale · "Twiy" · Written by the team
GalaxyScale Wanted Dual Domain
Appearance: Pink and purple scales that shift and catch light like the inside of a shell — depending on the angle she looks entirely different. A dab of royal blue on her tail. Rose-gold claws. And her eyes: look closely and you'll see what appears to be a birthmark in the shape of a star, a constellation of sorts living in her iris.

Power: Draws from the galaxy and stars themselves — light and dark in equal measure. During extreme emotions, she releases uncontrolled blasts of raw power and energy. As a GalaxyScale, she is one of the most dangerous dragons alive, and one of the rarest. Someone is hunting for her.

Status: Currently wanted by an unknown faction. The reason is not yet recorded in the official files.
The Shards

The most powerful dragon bloodlines in recorded history. Each Shard commands total dominion over a fundamental force of the world. To face one is not to fight a dragon — it is to fight nature itself. They are called Shards because the old scholars believed each one was broken off from something greater, a primordial power too vast for any single creature to contain whole.

🔥
Flame
Domain of Fire & Heat
Core Ability
A Flame Shard can burst their entire body into living fire — not a coating, not an aura, but a full transformation into something between dragon and star. The flames they produce burn at temperatures so extreme they can melt any known metal on contact, including dragon-forged alloys that take centuries to smelt. Stone becomes liquid glass. Ice evaporates before it can get close.
Extended Powers
Flame Shards can command fire from any source within a wide radius — campfires, lava vents, the heat of a forge — drawing them together into a weapon or shield. They are entirely fireproof, and prolonged proximity to other Shards causes their temperature to spike involuntarily. In battle, they are known to leave scorched glass footprints where they walk. The most powerful Flame Shards can ignite the air itself — not just set things aflame, but turn the atmosphere into a wave of burning pressure that knocks enemies flat before the fire even arrives.
Weakness
Extinguished by the Sea Shard — water and ice can suppress their flames, though a Flame Shard at full power fights back. In enough water, they become temporarily powerless. Wind can also scatter their fire if the Shard is unprepared, though it can be just as easily redirected back as a firestorm.
🌊
Sea
Domain of Water & Ice
Core Ability
Sea Shards command water in every form it takes — liquid, ice, and steam. They can call water from the air itself, pulling moisture from clouds or even dry stone when desperate. Their senses are so precisely attuned to water that they can detect any source within miles, hearing underground rivers, sensing rainfall before clouds appear, finding hidden pools beneath desert rock. What they find, they can control.
Extended Powers
They can freeze water instantly — turning a river into a wall, a wave into a cage, rain into a hail of razor-sharp ice shards. Advanced Sea Shards can freeze an enemy mid-motion by drawing the moisture from the air around them and solidifying it into a prison. They breathe underwater indefinitely. Their scales are impervious to drowning, pressure, and temperature extremes in water. Most terrifyingly, they can sense the water content of living things — and some have learned to use this knowledge in ways that are not recorded in public files.
Weakness
Countered by the Flame Shard's extreme heat, which can evaporate their water constructs faster than they can remake them. Wind can disrupt their wave-form attacks by scattering water into useless droplets. Crystal Shards can also partially contain them by pulling water down into earth.
🌑
Moon
Domain of Darkness & Perception
Core Ability
Moon Shards do not destroy — they unmake the ability to perceive. They can reach into an enemy's visual field and darken their sight completely, as if someone poured ink over the world. This lasts anywhere from seconds to minutes depending on the Moon Shard's strength and the victim's resistance. Worse, they can conjure spheres of pure, absolute darkness that expand outward like a held breath being released — swallowing areas whole, inside which nothing can be seen and sound behaves strangely.
Extended Powers
Moon Shards are connected to the lunar cycle. At the full moon, they are near-unstoppable. At a new moon, they are at their weakest. Beyond vision: they can muffle sound within their darkness zones, making communication between blinded enemies impossible. The most gifted Moon Shards have been recorded creating what scholars call a shadow self — a duplicate of themselves made from darkness that can move independently and confuse enemies during combat. They can see perfectly in any level of darkness and often hunt at night for this reason.
Weakness
Directly opposed by the Sun Shard — a single focused burst of Sun light can shatter a Moon Shard's darkness sphere entirely. Galaxy Shards can counter them because they carry both sides of this power. Moon Shards operating in broad daylight are significantly weakened and tend to close combat quickly.
💎
Crystal
Domain of Earth & Armour
Core Ability
Crystal Shards can manifest armour from nothing — pulling mineral matter from the earth, from stone underfoot, from the minerals dissolved in the very air, and forming it into seamless plates around themselves or an ally in seconds. This armour is not simple rock: it is crystalline, perfectly structured, harder than anything forged in a furnace. They can also command the earth itself — shift stone, raise walls, crack ground open.
Extended Powers
Advanced Crystal Shards can extend their armour outward into spikes, shields, and weapons — essentially wearing the battlefield itself as an extension of their body. They are notoriously difficult to kill because even if their armour shatters, they simply pull more. They can also sense structural weaknesses in anything solid — walls, armour on other dragons, natural formations — and know exactly where to strike to collapse them entirely. Some Crystal Shards have been recorded literally encasing opponents in crystal, freezing them mid-motion in a tomb of clear mineral.
Weakness
Their armour, while extraordinary, has limits under enough sustained Flame heat — crystal can eventually crack and melt. Sea Shards can undermine the earth beneath them, removing their footing and supply. Wind Shards find crystal armour difficult to penetrate directly but can use sheer force to tip an armoured Crystal Shard off balance.
🌪
Wind
Domain of Air & Motion
Core Ability
Wind Shards bend the air entirely to their will — from a gentle current capable of carrying a whisper miles away to a hurricane-force gale that levels forests. The wind is not just a weapon: it is a sixth sense. They can feel every disturbance in the air around them, detecting movement, approaching enemies, even reading emotional states through subtle changes in the air a creature exhales. They are almost impossible to ambush.
Extended Powers
Wind Shards can fly at speeds that blur their form, using air to launch themselves faster than most dragons can track. They can create localised vacuums — pockets where no air exists — which is as effective against fire as any water. They can redirect projectiles mid-flight, deflect other elemental attacks, and even strip the air from an enclosed area, which is as cruel as it sounds. Elite Wind Shards can create contained storms: vortexes that trap opponents, twisters that scour the ground, wind walls that deflect everything including sound.
Weakness
Crystal Shards can anchor themselves and others against wind, using earth as a counter-weight. Sea Shards can flood an area, grounding a Wind Shard's aerial advantage and turning their hurricane into rain. Flame Shards can use a Wind Shard's own gusts against them — redirecting their currents as superheated firestorms.
☀️
Sun
Domain of Light & Radiance
Core Ability
Sun Shards can unleash blinding flashes of concentrated light — not ordinary brightness, but focused photonic force that overwhelms the visual system entirely, leaving enemies sightless for minutes. They can also become a living beacon, radiating brilliant light visible from impossible distances — useful for signalling, for revealing hidden enemies, or simply for making it impossible to hide anything in their presence.
Extended Powers
Sun Shards draw power from sunlight itself, making them grow dramatically stronger at midday and in open terrain. They can focus their light into a narrow beam of concentrated solar energy — essentially a laser capable of burning through stone. Advanced Sun Shards can maintain light constructs: walls of radiance that hurt to approach, illuminated illusions that distract and confuse, or sustained beacons that operate independently while they fight. Some scholars believe the most powerful Sun Shards can actually accelerate plant growth and reverse some forms of poison using their focused light.
Weakness
Directly opposed by Moon Shards — their darkness spheres can absorb and nullify Sun Shard light. At night, a Sun Shard away from artificial light sources is severely weakened. Dense storm clouds (which a Sea or Wind Shard can produce) can cut off their solar power source mid-battle. They are also unable to use their light subtly — it is always visible, always announcing their location.

Galaxy
Domain of All Light & All Darkness — The Dual Shard
Moon Powers Sun Powers Dual Domain Extremely Rare Wanted
What Makes Them Different
Galaxy Shards do not have one domain. They have two — and they are not simply half of each. A Galaxy Shard contains the full power of the Moon and the full power of the Sun, held simultaneously in one body. Old scholars believed this should be impossible — that light and dark would cancel each other out. Instead, they compound. A Galaxy Shard can flood a battlefield with blinding radiance, then snuff every light source in existence in the same breath.
Extended Powers
Beyond the combined Moon and Sun abilities, Galaxy Shards develop something unique to their bloodline: spatial perception. Because they command both what can be seen and what cannot, they develop an awareness of the space between — the shapes of darkness, the angles of light. In practice this means they can detect cloaked or invisible things that even Moon Shards miss, track the path of light bending around obstacles, and in rare cases project both a blinding flash and an absolute darkness field at the same time to produce total sensory disorientation. The most advanced Galaxy Shards have been reported able to bend light around their own form, rendering themselves effectively invisible — not in darkness, but in plain sight.
Why They Are Hunted
No other Shard can fully counter a Galaxy Shard. Moon Shards cannot use darkness against them — they share it. Sun Shards cannot blind them — they invented it. Their only meaningful weaknesses are the same as either parent domain in isolation, and a skilled Galaxy Shard rotates between powers fast enough that neither applies. This is why they are rare. It may also be why they are wanted.
The Great Balance

Nothing in the world of the Forbidden Scales exists without its opposite. Every power has a counter. Every strength has a mirror. The ancient scholars called it the Correspondence — the belief that the world maintains itself not through peace, but through opposition. When one Shard rises unchecked, another is pulled toward it like the tide. This is not a coincidence. It is a law.

Flame
vs
Sea
The oldest opposition in the world. Flame burns at temperatures that can turn stone to glass — but Sea does not simply put fire out. A Sea Shard can freeze the very moisture in the air around a Flame Shard, cutting off the oxygen that feeds their fire, encasing them in ice before they can reach full temperature. Flame Shards at peak power can evaporate water faster than it can be replaced — but Sea Shards can call from the atmosphere itself, from underground rivers, from clouds miles away. The contest is not about which is hotter or colder. It is about which runs out first.

Conversely: Flame Shards can melt a Sea Shard's ice constructs instantly, turn their water shields to scalding steam, and render rivers useless by boiling them dry. A Sea Shard fighting a Flame Shard is constantly choosing: go bigger or go precise.
"The sea does not fear the fire. The sea simply remembers that fire needs air — and the sea has drowned things before they could ever burn." — Old scholar's marginalia, volume unknown.
Sun
vs
Moon
The most personal opposition. Sun and Moon Shards do not simply cancel each other's powers — they unravel each other's entire purpose. A Moon Shard's darkness sphere, which can engulf a battlefield and leave armies stumbling blind, is shattered instantly by a single concentrated Sun burst. A Sun Shard's blinding flash — terrifying against every other enemy — washes harmlessly over a Moon Shard who sees perfectly in absolute dark.

What makes this balance extraordinary is that neither side wins in isolation: in full daylight the Moon Shard is weakened; at night, the Sun Shard dims. They are calibrated against the world's own rhythm — and the only being that breaks this rule entirely is the Galaxy Shard, who carries the burden of both. Galaxy Shards are not a balance of Sun and Moon. They are what happens when the balance breaks — and the world does not correct it.
"Light makes darkness visible. Darkness makes light meaningful. Take either away and you do not get more of the other — you get blindness of a different kind." — Recovered text, author unknown.
Wind
vs
Crystal
The opposition between the untethered and the immovable. Wind is formless, invisible, and omnipresent — it cannot be grabbed, held, or permanently blocked. Crystal is the opposite: dense, permanent, built to endure. A Wind Shard can shatter a Crystal Shard's armour with enough sustained force — stone cracks under pressure if you find the right angle, and Crystal Shards can sense structural weaknesses in everything except air. But a Crystal Shard can anchor itself, drive spikes into the ground, and become impossible to lift, topple, or throw — which is how a Wind Shard prefers to fight.

The tension in this pairing is mobility vs durability. Wind Shards are fastest. Crystal Shards are most protected. In a long fight, Crystal wins. In a fast fight, Wind wins. This is why Wind Shards never let a fight go long.
"The mountain does not chase the wind. It does not need to. It is still standing when every storm has passed." — Crystal Shard battle philosophy, traditional.
The Correspondence — Full Wheel
The six base Shards form what old scholars called the Wheel of Correspondence — no single point has no counter, and no two adjacent powers are without tension between them.

Flame suppresses Sea · Sea suppresses Flame
Sun suppresses Moon · Moon suppresses Sun
Wind challenges Crystal · Crystal challenges Wind

But the wheel also turns across pairs: Flame and Wind compound — fire feeds on air, wind spreads flame into an inferno. Sea and Crystal reinforce — water carves channels in earth, and the earth holds water in place. Sun and Flame share warmth — neither harms the other, and together they are relentless. Moon and Sea share tides — Sea Shards near Moon Shards report increased range and sensitivity.
The Galaxy Exception
The Galaxy Shard sits outside the Wheel entirely. Scholars originally placed it at the centre — commanding both light and dark, it seemed like a mediator, a balance-keeper. The more recent theory is grimmer: the Galaxy Shard does not maintain balance. It is what happens when balance is absent. Every century or so, when the Wheel tips badly — when one Shard bloodline grows too strong, or another is nearly wiped out — a Galaxy Shard appears. Not as a ruler. As a correction. Whether this is comforting or terrifying depends on which side of the imbalance you are standing on.

The Wolf Pack

The Red Wolf pack — 28 strong, led by Fang and Storm. Bonded to the dragon companions through ancient instinct and hard-won trust. Not pets. Not soldiers. Partners.

Fang — Alpha Male
Blue-coated wolf. Dark gold eyes with orange-red flecks. Silver claws, silver-tipped tail. Vegan — loves berries, sugarcane, and vegetables. Can run at the speed of sound for 5,000 miles before slowing. Can sense Topaz's location at any distance and detect any animal within a 10-mile radius. Can growl dragon-like words. Former Alpha of the Red Wolves — his leadership is both earned and absolute. Has photographic memory. Can appear to teleport when moving side to side at full speed. Can call his entire pack across the continent with a single howl.
Freki
Main Healer
Dusky blue and silver coat, white underbelly, dusky red ear tips. Deeply loyal to Storm. Loves herbs, pups, and rolling in snow. Photographic memory for plants. Can vaguely speak Dragon. Can run 35–40 mph for 450 miles. Will fight for her pack if she must.
Echo
Head of Defense
Silky red fur, black splotches, deep piercing black eyes. Strong and confident — many respect her, others fear her. Loud howl, exceptional stealth. Loyal to Fang above all. Not trusting of strangers, but follows orders without complaint.
Chair
Scout Leader
Whitish-red fur, maroon splotches. Serious to a fault, despises pups, bossy and prone to snapping. In his free time he sits lazily — which is, in fact, exactly why he is named Chair. He trusts his scouts. He just doesn't act like it.
Bolt
Gatherer Leader
Red-tinted green fur, brownish-maroon underbelly, leaf-shaped brown markings. Extremely fast and resourceful. Prefers solitude but has a genuine soft spot for Hail. Has anger issues. Dislikes loud noises and sudden movements — they make him lash out.
Aspen
Hunter Leader
Bright red fur, black underbelly, heterochromia — brown left eye, yellow right. Kind but results-driven. Hates procrastination. Likes Kodiak. Gets along with healers and caretakers. In her free time she carves wood into small wolf figures.
Hail
Caretaker Leader
Light blue fur with red flecks, deep golden-brown eyes, silver-tipped tail, retractable teal claws. Protective beyond reason, incredibly smart. Fang wants her as a scout. She wants to be with the pups. Specifically Taco, who only listens to her.

World Rules — The Law of the Scales

  • No mythical beings except dragons
  • All characters must fit established body limits for their Shard type

  • Pets remain companions — they do not become the main characters
